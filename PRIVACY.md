## Privacy Policy — Hansel
## GrimmsTales

Hansel collects GPS location, altitude, and timestamp data
continuously, including while the device screen is off, in
order to record changing altitude of hiking tracks in active
volcanic terrain from week to week, for scientific
documentation.  This location data is stored locally on the
device only and is not currently transmitted, shared, or
sold to any third party.  A future version of Hansel may add
an optional feature to upload logged data to cloud storage
under the user's control; this policy will be updated before
that feature ships.
